No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies, doesn't count visitors and loads nothing from other servers: fonts, images, the video and scripts all come from the same server as the page. There are no analytics or advertising services and no embedded content from third parties.

There's one small exception. When you click a language link (such as "Deutsch" or "English"), your browser remembers that choice in its local storage (localStorage, key lang), so the start page opens in your language next time. This entry never leaves your browser, and you can delete it at any time in your browser settings. Under Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG), this needs no consent, because the entry only stores the language you chose.

We use Brevo for this, a service of Brevo SAS, 9-17 rue Salneuve, 75017 Paris, France. The form sends your details straight to Brevo; your browser only contacts Brevo when you submit it. Brevo processes the data in the EU, mainly in France and Belgium. Some of Brevo's sub-processors are outside the EU; for them, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses apply (see the list in Brevo's data processing agreement, Appendix 3 of its terms of use). The page you see after clicking the confirmation link is served by Brevo, and Brevo's privacy policy applies there. Giving your email address is voluntary; if you don't, you just won't get the notification.

Double opt-in: after you sign up, you'll get an email with a confirmation link. You're only on the list once you've clicked it. Brevo records when you signed up and confirmed, along with your IP address, so that your consent can be proven later.

Legal basis: your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time, using the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to ralf.d.mueller@gmail.com. Withdrawing your consent only affects the future; it doesn't make any processing before that unlawful.

How long we keep it: until you unsubscribe or we close the list. If you never confirm your sign-up, your address isn't added to the list; the confirmation link expires after 30 days. After you unsubscribe, we delete your address; we keep proof of your earlier consent only as long as the law requires.

Data processing agreement: we have a data processing agreement with Brevo under Art. 28 GDPR. Brevo processes the data only on our instructions.

Statistics: Brevo counts how often emails are opened and links in them are clicked. This counting is set to anonymous: opens and clicks aren't linked to any individual person.

For more, see Brevo's privacy policy.
