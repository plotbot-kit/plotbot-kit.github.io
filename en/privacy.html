No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies, doesn't count visitors and loads nothing from other servers: fonts, images, the video and scripts all come from the same server as the page. There are no analytics or advertising services and no embedded content from third parties.

There's one small exception. When you click "Deutsch" or "English" at the top, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage (localStorage, key lang), so the start page opens in your language next time. This entry never leaves your browser, and you can delete it at any time in your browser settings. The legal basis is Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG: the entry is needed to store the language you chose.

Legal basis: your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time, using the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to [EMAIL]. Withdrawal applies from then on; it doesn't affect processing that took place before.
