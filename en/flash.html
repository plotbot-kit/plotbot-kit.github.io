Flash the firmware

Here's how to load PlotBot's firmware onto its ESP32 board, straight from your browser. You don't need to install anything. The flashing is done by ESP Launchpad, a web page run by Espressif, the company that makes the ESP32 chip. After that, you control PlotBot from your phone, and later updates go through its phone page.

Firmware version 0.30.1

What you need

  • A computer with Chrome or Edge. Launchpad needs a browser that can talk to the board over USB (Web Serial); Safari can't.
  • A USB data cable. Some cables only charge and don't carry data. If your computer doesn't detect the board, the cable is the usual suspect.
  • On Windows, you may need the driver for the CP2102 USB chip. Windows usually installs it by itself. If the board doesn't show up in the list, download the “CP210x USB to UART Bridge VCP Driver” from Silicon Labs.

How it works

  1. Plug the ESP32 board into your computer with the USB cable.
  2. Click “Flash the firmware” below. ESP Launchpad opens in a new tab and shows “PlotBot” with the firmware's version number.
  3. There, click “Connect Your Device”. Your browser shows a list. Pick the entry that says “CP210x” or “USB to UART” and click “Connect”.
  4. Launchpad doesn't ask again: it erases the board's memory and writes the firmware. The black “Device Console” window shows the progress. This takes a minute or two.
  5. PlotBot then restarts and opens its own Wi-Fi network. The “Device Console” shows a line starting with “Mein WLAN:” (German for “my Wi-Fi”) and the network's name. You can unplug the cable now.
Flash the firmware

The link takes you to ESP Launchpad on espressif.github.io. That page belongs to Espressif, not to us. It downloads the firmware from our website and sends it over the cable to the board. Our privacy policy explains what that means for your data.

Launchpad erases the board every time

Before it writes the firmware, Launchpad erases the board's entire memory. The first time, that doesn't matter. Once PlotBot has been drawing, though, erasing wipes its calibration and its settings. So install updates from PlotBot's phone page instead: that keeps both.

If it doesn't work

  • If Launchpad reports “Connection In Progress” or “Connection Error”, click “Try Again” and connect once more. This time, hold the BOOT button on the board as soon as you've picked the port, and let go once the progress starts.
  • If the board isn't in the list, check the cable and the driver (see above).
  • If Launchpad says “Your browser of choice doesn't support the Web Serial API”, open this page on a computer in Chrome or Edge.
  • Launchpad's error window includes a “Troubleshooting Guide” with more tips, for example on serial port permissions under Linux and the driver on Windows.
  • You can also flash the firmware from the command line, without a browser. The PlotBot-Kit book shows you how.
