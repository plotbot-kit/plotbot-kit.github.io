Flash the firmware

This page loads PlotBot's firmware onto its ESP32 board, straight from your browser. You don't need to install anything. After that, you control PlotBot from your phone, and later updates go through its phone page.

Firmware version 0.30.1

What you need

  • A computer with Chrome or Edge – that's the setup we've tested. Safari can't talk to the board.
  • A USB data cable. Some cables only charge and don't carry data. If your computer doesn't detect the board, the cable is the usual suspect.
  • On Windows, you may need the driver for the CP2102 USB chip. Windows usually installs it by itself. If the board doesn't show up in the list, download the “CP210x USB to UART Bridge VCP Driver” from Silicon Labs.

How it works

  1. Plug the ESP32 board into your computer with the USB cable.
  2. Click “Flash the firmware” below. Your browser shows a list. Pick the entry that says “CP210x” or “USB to UART” and click “Connect”.
  3. Click “Install PlotBot”.
  4. You're asked whether to erase the device (“Erase device”). The first time, it's fine to tick the box. If you flash the firmware again later, leave it unticked – otherwise you'd lose the calibration. Then click “Next”.
  5. Confirm with “Install”.
  6. Wait for “Installation complete!”. It takes about a minute. PlotBot then restarts and opens its own Wi-Fi network.
This browser can't talk to the board. Open this page on a computer in Chrome or Edge. Your browser doesn't allow the connection here. Open the page at https://plotbot-kit.github.io/.

The button only appears with JavaScript turned on. The firmware goes from your browser over the cable straight to the board; no data leaves your computer.

If it doesn't work

  • If the connection drops, hold the BOOT button on the board while you click “Install”, and let go once the progress bar moves.
  • If the board isn't in the list, check the cable and the driver (see above).
  • You can also flash the firmware from the command line, without a browser. The PlotBot-Kit book shows you how.
